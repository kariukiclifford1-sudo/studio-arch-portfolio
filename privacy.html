Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains what personal information Studio Arch collects through this website, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

Who we are

Studio Arch (Doris Nganga) is the owner of this website and decides how your information is used. You can reach us at dorisnganga044@gmail.com or on +254743525095.

What we collect

When you use the consultation form we collect:

  • your name and email address
  • the type of project you are interested in
  • the message you write about your project

If you email, call or message us on WhatsApp, we also keep that conversation so we can reply and follow up.

Why we collect it

We use your information only to reply to your request, discuss your project and, if you decide to work with us, deliver our services. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for mass advertising.

Who can see it

The consultation form is received through Netlify, the company that hosts this website. They process the form on our behalf. We may also share information with people who help deliver your project (for example a consultant or contractor), but only when you agree and only what is needed.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries only as long as needed to respond and to follow up on a project. If an enquiry does not lead to a project, we delete it when it is no longer useful.

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you
  • correct information that is wrong
  • delete your information
  • stop using your information for a purpose you do not agree with

Send your request to dorisnganga044@gmail.com. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

Cookies and browser storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It remembers the page you last viewed in your browser's local storage for up to one hour, so you can continue where you stopped. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Links to other sites

Our pages link to Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook and WhatsApp. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
